.zmx
Sequential files supported

Ray diagrams are approximate and can be wrong. Layouts and rays are drawn with first-order (paraxial) optics only — no real ray tracing. Folded systems are shown unfolded, and designs using solves, pickups or model glasses may render or compute incorrectly. Always verify against OpticStudio before relying on anything here.

Your designs never leave your device. This page has no server, no database and no analytics. Your file is opened locally in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, logged or transmitted anywhere. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking. Close the tab and nothing remains — you can even disconnect from the internet and it will keep working.

Reads Zemax sequential lens files entirely in your browser.
